How to play

Connect an Atlantic state to a Pacific state — or the other way around — with the 24 cards in your deck.

  1. Start on a coast. Choose a card marked COAST. Gulf-only states don't count.
  2. Follow the borders. Click a neighboring card to extend your route. Click a played card in your hand or on the map to remove it and everything after it.
  3. Reach the other coast. The game ends automatically. Cards are worth 1–4 points.

Each invalid card selection deducts that state's original point value, even after a hint. Repeated mistakes deduct points again. Undoing cards is free. Scores can go negative.

Hint highlights the next card, or a card to remove if the route is blocked. After using Hint, the next card you add scores 0, even if you choose a different card. That card stays at 0 if removed and replayed. There is no time limit.

Every deal is solvable. Borders include AZ–CO, NM–UT, MI–MN and NY–RI.
